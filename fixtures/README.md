receipts generated by `src/write-fixture.mjs`. They contain 16 successful
streams, final usage objects, relative timings, and `SSE CHUNK MODE` labels.

They are deterministic demo data, not a hardware measurement or a claim about
any private endpoint. Regenerate them with:

